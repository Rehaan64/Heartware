# 💖 Heartware
A lightweight, aesthetic desktop overlay widget that monitors your system telemetry while acting as a living, time-aware Tamagotchi companion.

Features

* **🩺 Live Metabolism (BPM):** Your system's workload dynamically translates into a beating heart rate. The heavier your hardware works, the faster your companion's pulse races!
* **🧠 Advanced Telemetry:** Real-time tracking for **CPU Load**, **RAM Usage**, **GPU Utilization**, **VRAM**, **Temperatures**, and system thermal status.
* **🐾 Tamagotchi Personality:** Features dynamic, time-aware moods and facial expressions. If your PC runs hot or stays active late into the night, your companion will let you know!
* **🎨 Multiple Skins & Themes:** Switch seamlessly between built-in themes (*Default*, *Cyberpunk*, *Matrix*) via a clean context menu.
* **🪶 Non-Intrusive Overlay:** Runs as a borderless, top-most desktop widget that expands on hover and stays completely out of your way during work or gaming.
* **🛠️ Quick Controls:** Right-click anywhere on the widget to copy a status log, cycle themes, or exit instantly.
